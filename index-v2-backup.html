What I found

01

Your phone number isn't on your own page.

I found no phone number and no tap-to-call link anywhere on eskrimalasvegas.com. The page tells visitors to use the contact form or your social media, and the form's confirmation says to check spam if there's no answer within five business days. Your number, 626-324-2320, is on your chapter page at edcfma.com, and it's the page my search for your club's name quoted it from. I checked this in a desktop browser, not on a real phone.

02

The first photo takes about 24 seconds to finish on a slow-phone simulation.

On two Lighthouse runs (a lab simulation of a phone on a slow connection) the largest item on the first screen, the photo of John Dacpano Jr., finished at 23.7 and 23.9 seconds, and the performance score was 52 and 55. The page weighs about 4.2 MB: the group photo in your gallery is 1.8 MB and that first photo is 0.7 MB. Lighthouse estimates about 2.4 MB could be saved with modern image formats. That is a lab number, not a measurement of your visitors.

03

The page has no search description and no language setting.

Lighthouse flagged a missing meta description and a missing language attribute on the page, and scored SEO 82. The home page has no business markup, no robots.txt and no sitemap: all three return nothing. Without a description, Google writes its own snippet from the page text.

04

Pinch-zoom is switched off and some pictures and icons have no names.

The page's viewport setting includes user-scalable=no, which stops visitors from zooming in on a phone. Four gallery images have no alt text, and the round social-media icons in the footer are links with no readable name, so a screen reader announces only “link”. Headings also skip levels. Lighthouse scored accessibility 73.

05

The page doesn't say where, when or how much.

It says training is by appointment, as online classes, private sessions and small groups, and that the club is public park, garage and backyard based. It gives no meeting place, no class times and no prices. In a search for eskrima classes in Las Vegas, the schools the search named were ones that publish a street address, a schedule or a monthly price.

06

Your one film is a YouTube embed that adds about 480 KB.

The home page embeds one YouTube video. Loading its player added about 482 KB of YouTube code to the page in the Lighthouse run, and the gallery under it is photos only. A short clip of a class could sit near the top, next to the way to get started.

What's working

The page says plainly what you teach: Eskrima, Arnis, Kali, Tang Soo Do and Koshiki ToKen Jutsu, with a clear history of each and your own story as an instructor. The text is real page text, about 1,680 words in the HTML the server sends, the page has a title, and the contact form delivered the message I sent through it. Lighthouse lab scores: performance 52 and 55, accessibility 73, best practices 96, SEO 82.

Unmeasured

Rankings, visitor numbers, your Google listing, and your Facebook, Instagram and YouTube, which I didn't view. A real phone: I tested the page width in a desktop browser only. The site is a single page, and I opened that one page.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    AI crawlers aren't blocked

    You have no robots.txt (it returns 404), so nothing there keeps GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, OAI-SearchBot or CCBot from reading the site.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The HTML the server sends carries about 1,680 words and the heading Eskrima DeCuerdas Las Vegas, Nevada.

  • Gap
    No sitemap and no llms.txt

    /sitemap.xml and /llms.txt both return 404, so crawlers and AI tools have no list of your pages.

  • Gap
    No business markup

    The home page has no structured data: no business name, phone, address, hours or social profiles.

  • Gap
    No phone, address or hours on the page

    A tool that reads only the page can't give a visitor a way to reach you or a place to train.

  • Note
    Your chapter page lists the number

    edcfma.com lists 626-324-2320 and says lessons are by appointment. The same details aren't on your own page.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 18, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.
